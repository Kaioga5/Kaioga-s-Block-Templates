**The public license.** Everyone gets the [PolyForm Noncommercial License
1.0.0](LICENSE). Under it you may use, study, modify, build on and redistribute
the templates for any noncommercial purpose, at no cost and without asking.

**The commercial license.** The public license grants no commercial rights at
all. If you want to use the templates commercially, you need a separate license
from Kaioga, agreed in writing before you use them that way. These are handled
case by case, so ask rather than assume.

## What generally needs a commercial license

This section is guidance, not license terms. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) is what
actually governs, and if the two ever read differently, the LICENSE wins.

Ask first if you plan to:

- ship a template, or a block built from one, in a paid add-on, map, world,
  skin pack or Marketplace product
- include the templates in a paid course, paid tutorial or paid asset pack
- use them in work you are being paid to deliver for a client
- use them inside a company, studio or other for-profit organisation
- sell, sublicense or rent the templates, changed or unchanged

Personal projects, learning, experiments, hobby add-ons, free public releases,
and use by schools, charities and other noncommercial organisations are already
covered by the public license. The license spells this out under *Personal Uses*
and *Noncommercial Organizations*.

If your case sits somewhere in between, ask. It is a short conversation and it
costs nothing.

## Asking for a commercial license

Contact the author, Kaioga, through GitHub -- either by opening an issue on
[the repository](https://github.com/Kaioga5/Kaioga-s-Block-Templates) or through
the [profile page](https://github.com/Kaioga5).

It helps to say up front:

- which templates you want to use
- what you are building and where it will be published
- whether you are an individual, a team or a company

## Contributions

Because the commercial license is granted by the author alone, contributions
need to leave the author able to grant it. By opening a pull request you confirm
the work is yours to give and that Kaioga may license it under both the public
noncommercial license and separate commercial licenses. Pull requests that
cannot meet that will not be merged.

## Scope

A commercial license from Kaioga covers only the material Kaioga owns. It does
not grant any right in Minecraft, the Bedrock add-on platform or Mojang's own
assets. Publishing Minecraft content stays subject to Mojang's terms either way.
See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
